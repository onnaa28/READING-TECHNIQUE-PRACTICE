SKIMMING - Definition

Skimming is a reading technique used to get the general idea or overall meaning of a text quickly. When we skim a text, we do not read every single word carefully. Instead, we focus on the most important parts, such as the title, headings, first sentences, last sentences, keywords, and repeated ideas.

Example: Imagine you receive a 5-page article about environmental problems. Your teacher asks, "What is this article mainly about?" You don't need to read every sentence—just read the title, headings, and key sentences.

How to use Skimming:

  • 1. Read the title.
  • 2. Look at headings & subheadings.
  • 3. Read the 1st sentence of each paragraph.
  • 4. Look for repeated keywords.
  • 5. Pay attention to the conclusion.
  • 6. Decide the general topic of the text.
Soal

SKIMMING - Example 1

Passage:

Sarah's English teacher gave the students a four-page article entitled "Healthy Lifestyle for Teenagers." The article discussed several topics, including healthy eating, regular exercise, getting enough sleep, drinking enough water, and managing stress.

Sarah only had three minutes before the class discussion started. Instead of reading every sentence carefully, she first looked at the title. Then, she read the headings and the first sentences of several paragraphs. She noticed that the article repeatedly discussed ways teenagers could improve their physical and mental health. After doing this, Sarah was able to explain that the article was generally about healthy habits that teenagers should develop in their daily lives.

Question:

Which reading technique did Sarah use?

A. Scanning
B. Reading for Detail
C. Inference
D. Skimming
E. Vocabulary in Context
Pembahasan

Pembahasan: SKIMMING - Example 1

✅ Jawaban: D. Skimming

Alasan: Jawabannya adalah D. Skimming karena Sarah hanya melihat judul (title), subjudul (headings), dan kalimat pertama dari setiap paragraf. Ia tidak membaca seluruh artikel kata demi kata. Tujuannya adalah untuk memahami topik umum artikel secara cepat.

Tindakan khas Skimming yang dilakukan Sarah dalam teks:

  • Looked at the title
  • Read the headings
  • Read the first sentences
  • Understood that the article was generally about healthy habits
Soal

SKIMMING - Example 2

Reading Passage:

Many students experience stress because of school assignments, examinations, extracurricular activities, and social relationships. A small amount of stress can sometimes encourage students to prepare more seriously for an examination. However, excessive stress may affect their concentration, sleep, and emotional well-being.

There are several ways students can manage stress. They can create a realistic study schedule, take short breaks while studying, exercise regularly, get enough sleep, and talk to someone they trust when they feel overwhelmed. Spending time on enjoyable activities can also help students relax.

Learning how to manage stress is important because students need to maintain a balance between academic responsibilities and their personal lives.

Question (Pilihan Ganda Kompleks):

If a student wants to skim this passage, which information should they focus on?

A. The general topic of student stress
B. Every example of a stressful situation
C. The main ways students can manage stress
D. The exact number of students who experience stress
E. The overall relationship between stress and student well-being
Pembahasan

Pembahasan: SKIMMING - Example 2

✅ Jawaban Benar: A, C, E

A. The general topic of student stress - BENAR

Seluruh bacaan membahas tentang stres pada siswa, efeknya, dan cara mengelolanya. Ini fokus utama saat skimming.

C. The main ways students can manage stress - BENAR

Paragraf kedua menjelaskan cara-cara utama mengelola stres. Poin utama ini penting dicatat saat skimming.

E. The overall relationship between stress and student well-being - BENAR

Teks menjelaskan bahwa stres berlebih mempengaruhi konsentrasi dan kesejahteraan emosional. Ini bagian dari pesan keseluruhan.

SCANNING - Definition

Scanning is a reading technique used to find specific information quickly. Unlike skimming, scanning does not focus on the general meaning of a text. Instead, we search for a particular piece of information, such as a name, date, time, number, place, price, or specific fact.

Example: If you read an announcement and the teacher asks "When will the competition begin?", you don't need to read everything. You simply look for time-related numbers/words.

How to use Scanning:

  1. Read the question first.
  2. Identify exact info needed.
  3. Think about keywords.
  4. Quickly search keywords in text.
  5. Read surrounding sentence to confirm answer.
Soal

SCANNING - Example 1

Passage:

Green Valley High School will hold its annual English Festival on Saturday, October 18. The event will take place in the school hall from 8:00 a.m. until 2:00 p.m. Students from grades 10, 11, and 12 are invited to participate. There will be several competitions, including a speech contest, storytelling competition, spelling bee, and English poster competition. Students must register between September 20 and October 5. The registration fee is Rp25,000 per participant, and registration can be completed in Room 12. The storytelling competition will begin at 10:30 a.m. Participants are expected to arrive at least thirty minutes before the competition begins.

Andi is interested in the storytelling competition. He wants to know exactly when the competition will begin.

Question:

Which reading technique should Andi use to find the information he needs?

A. Skimming
B. Scanning
C. Inference
D. Reading for Main Idea
E. Vocabulary in Context
Pembahasan

Pembahasan: SCANNING - Example 1

✅ Jawaban: B. Scanning

Alasan: Jawabannya adalah B. Scanning karena Andi ingin menemukan satu informasi spesifik, yaitu jam mulai lomba storytelling.

Bukti dalam teks:

"The storytelling competition will begin at 10:30 a.m."

Andi tidak perlu memahami seluruh isi pengumuman, ia hanya perlu memindai (scan) detail jam saja.

Soal

READING FOR MAIN IDEA

Reading for Main Idea is used to identify the most important idea, central point, or overall message of a paragraph or passage.

Main Idea vs Supporting Details

Penulis biasanya memberikan 1 Gagasan Utama (Main Idea) yang didukung oleh beberapa Detail Pendukung (contoh, fakta, alasan).

Letak Main Idea

Bisa di awal, tengah, atau akhir paragraf. Kadang tersurat langsung, kadang tersirat (perlu disimpulkan).

Contoh Singkat:

"Exercise is important for students. Regular physical activity improves physical health, increases concentration, and reduces stress..."

Main Idea: Exercise provides several benefits for students.

Soal

MAIN IDEA - Example 1

Passage:

Technology has become an important part of teenagers' daily lives. Students can use smartphones, computers, and tablets to search for information, communicate with classmates, attend online lessons, and complete school assignments. Technology can therefore make learning more convenient and provide students with access to many educational resources.

However, excessive use of technology can also cause problems. Some teenagers spend many hours watching videos, playing games, or using social media. They may stay up late and get less sleep. They may also spend less time exercising, reading books, or communicating directly with their families.

For this reason, teenagers should learn to use technology responsibly. They do not necessarily need to stop using technology, but they should manage their screen time and make sure that technology does not interfere with sleep, physical activity, studying, and relationships with other people.

Question:

What is the main idea of the passage?

A. Teenagers should stop using smartphones completely.
B. Technology is only useful for entertainment.
C. Teenagers should use technology responsibly and maintain a healthy balance.
D. Social media is the biggest problem for teenagers.
E. Students should spend most of their time using technology for school.
Pembahasan

Pembahasan: MAIN IDEA - Example 1

✅ Jawaban: C. Teenagers should use technology responsibly and maintain a healthy balance.

Alasan: Seluruh teks membahas bagaimana teknologi itu berguna namun bisa menimbulkan masalah jika digunakan secara berlebihan.

Pesan Utama Paragraf Terakhir:

"Teenagers should learn to use technology responsibly and manage their screen time."

Gagasan ini menghubungkan seluruh informasi dari ketiga paragraf secara utuh.

Soal

MAIN IDEA - Example 2

Passage:

Reading books regularly can provide many benefits for students. Reading can increase vocabulary because students encounter unfamiliar words in different contexts. It can also improve students' ability to understand complex ideas and follow longer explanations.

In addition, reading can help students develop their imagination. When reading stories, students may imagine characters, places, and events described by the writer. Reading can also encourage students to spend less time using digital devices for entertainment.

For these reasons, developing a regular reading habit can support students' academic development as well as their personal growth.

Question (Pilihan Ganda Kompleks):

Which statements represent the main ideas / key points of the passage?

A. Reading can improve students' vocabulary.
B. Reading can help students understand complex ideas.
C. Reading can develop imagination.
D. Reading regularly provides several benefits for students.
E. Reading can replace all forms of digital entertainment.
Pembahasan

Pembahasan: MAIN IDEA - Example 2

✅ Jawaban Benar: A, B, C, D

A (BENAR): Teks menyatakan membaca meningkatkan kosakata.
B (BENAR): Membaca membantu memahami ide kompleks.
C (BENAR): Membaca dapat mengembangkan imajinasi.
D (BENAR): Merekap gagasan utama bahwa membaca memberikan banyak manfaat.
E (SALAH): Teks tidak pernah menyatakan bahwa membaca harus menggantikan semua bentuk hiburan digital.

READING FOR DETAIL

Reading for Detail is used to understand specific information and important facts in a text. It requires close attention to the information presented.

Purpose:

  • To find specific facts in a text.
  • To understand information accurately.
  • To answer detailed comprehension questions.
  • To avoid missing important information.

How to Read for Detail:

  1. Read carefully from beginning to end.
  2. Pay attention to names, dates, numbers.
  3. Look for answers to who, what, when, where, why, how.
  4. Ensure answer is directly supported by text.
Soal

READING FOR DETAIL - Ex 1

Passage:

Last month, Sunrise High School organized a five-day environmental program called "Green School Week." The program was held from Monday, March 10, until Friday, March 14.

On Monday, students cleaned classrooms and corridors. On Tuesday, they planted flowers and small trees. On Wednesday, students collected plastic bottles and other recyclable materials. On Thursday, the student council organized an environmental seminar. On Friday, students presented posters.

Question:

What activity did the students do on Wednesday?

A. They cleaned the school corridors.
B. They planted flowers and trees.
C. They collected recyclable materials.
D. They attended an environmental seminar.
E. They presented environmental posters.
Pembahasan

Pembahasan: DETAIL - Example 1

✅ Jawaban: C. They collected recyclable materials.

Alasan: Teks secara spesifik menyatakan kegiatan pada hari Rabu:

"On Wednesday, students collected plastic bottles and other recyclable materials."

Karena pertanyaan menanyakan tentang hari Rabu (Wednesday), kita langsung mencari detail rinci terkait hari tersebut.

Soal

Last semester, Green Valley High School organized "Share and Care" for three weeks (August 4-24). First week: collected storybooks, notebooks, clothes, packaged food (no money). INFERENCE - Definition

Inference means making a logical conclusion based on information or clues given in the text. Writers do not always tell us everything directly.

Clues from text + Logical thinking = Inference

Example:

"Daniel looked outside and saw dark clouds. He quickly closed all windows and brought clothes inside."

Teks tidak mengatakan "Akan turun hujan". Tapi kita bisa menyimpulkan (infer) bahwa hujan akan turun dari petunjuk awan gelap & tindakan Daniel.

Soal

INFERENCE - Example 1

Passage:

When Emily arrived at school on Monday morning, she noticed many students carrying umbrellas. Sky was dark, strong wind was blowing. During 1st lesson, heavy rain hit the windows. At break, students stayed inside and the yard was empty. Teachers checked windows and doors. Emily called her father to pick her up instead of walking home.

Question:

What can the reader infer from the passage?

A. The school was closed because of the weather.
B. Emily forgot to bring her umbrella.
C. The weather was probably very bad that day.
D. The students did not want to attend school.
E. The teachers planned to send everyone home.
Pembahasan

Pembahasan: INFERENCE - Example 1

✅ Jawaban: C. The weather was probably very bad that day.

Alasan: Petunjuk seperti payung, langit gelap, angin kencang, hujan deras, lapangan kosong, dan Emily minta dijemput menunjukkan kesimpulan logis bahwa cuaca hari itu sangat buruk.

Soal

INFERENCE - Example 2

Passage:

Rina usually arrived 15 minutes early, but today she was 30 minutes late. Her hair was slightly wet, carrying shoes in hand, with water drops on her uniform. She explained her bus came late and heavy rain started while waiting under a small roof.

Question (Pilihan Ganda Kompleks):

What can reasonably be inferred from the passage?

A. It was raining heavily that morning.
B. Rina probably had difficulty traveling to school.
C. Rina intentionally arrived late at school.
D. Rina's shoes may have become wet.
E. The school was closed because of the rain.
Pembahasan

Pembahasan: INFERENCE - Example 2

✅ Jawaban Benar: A, B, D

A (BENAR): Teks langsung menyebutkan "it started raining heavily".
B (BENAR): Bus terlambat dan hujan deras membuktikan perjalanan Rina sulit.
D (BENAR): Rina melepas dan memegang sepatunya sementara rambut dan seragamnya basah.

REFERENCE - Definition

Reference is the ability to identify what a pronoun or reference word refers to in a text (e.g., he, she, it, they, them, this, that, these, those).

Example:

"Lisa bought a new notebook because she needed it for her English assignment."

  • She → Lisa
  • It → the notebook
Soal

REFERENCE - Example 1

Passage:

The school library recently received several new books. Mrs. Anderson, the librarian, placed the books on a special shelf near the entrance. She also created a list of the new books so students could easily find the titles...

Question:

What does the word "She" in the second paragraph refer to?

A. The school
B. The reading program
C. Mrs. Anderson
D. A former student
E. The library entrance
Pembahasan

Pembahasan: REFERENCE - Example 1

✅ Jawaban: C. Mrs. Anderson

Alasan: Kata "She" muncul tepat setelah menyebutkan nama Mrs. Anderson (seorang wanita). Oleh karena itu, kata ganti "She" merujuk kepada Mrs. Anderson.

VOCABULARY IN CONTEXT

Vocabulary in Context means understanding the meaning of an unfamiliar word by looking at clues in the surrounding text without immediately opening a dictionary.

Common Context Clues:

  • Definition / Explanation
  • Examples
  • Synonyms or Antonyms
  • Description

Example:

"The road was slippery because it rained heavily. People walked carefully to avoid falling."

Clue "avoid falling" & "rain" → Slippery = licin/basah.

Soal

VOCABULARY IN CONTEXT - Ex 1

Passage:

Their science teacher reminded them to be careful when using the equipment. Some instruments were fragile, meaning that they could easily break if students handled them carelessly.

Question:

What is the closest meaning of the word "fragile"?

A. Very expensive
B. Easily broken
C. Very modern
D. Difficult to use
E. Extremely large
Pembahasan

Pembahasan: VOCABULARY - Ex 1

✅ Jawaban: B. Easily broken

Alasan: Teks memberikan petunjuk langsung berupa definisi:

"...fragile, meaning that they could easily break..."

Frasa "could easily break" secara langsung mendefinisikan arti dari kata "fragile".
